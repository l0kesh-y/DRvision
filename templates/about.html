About DRVision

An explainable deep learning pipeline for automated 5-class diabetic retinopathy severity grading, combining clinically-validated retinal preprocessing, DenseNet-121 + CBAM attention networks, and Grad-CAM lesion explainability.

Diagnostic Pipeline Architecture

1. Retinal Preprocessing

Circular optic disc masking, Ben Graham illumination normalization, and CLAHE contrast enhancement on the L-channel.

2. Deep Learning

DenseNet-121 backbone with Convolutional Block Attention Module (CBAM) for spatial and channel-wise feature refinement.

3. Grad-CAM Explainability

Multi-channel gradient attention maps highlighting microaneurysms, dot/blot hemorrhages, and hard exudates.

4. Clinical Reporting

Continuous severity scores, International Clinical DR Scale (Grades 0–4), risk triage, and referral recommendations.

International Clinical DR Severity Scale

Grade Classification Key Pathological Findings Risk Level
0 No DR No visible abnormalities across all quadrants. Low
1 Mild NPDR Microaneurysms only; no hard exudates or hemorrhages. Low
2 Moderate NPDR More than microaneurysms: dot/blot hemorrhages, hard exudates, cotton-wool spots. Moderate
3 Severe NPDR 4-2-1 Rule: >20 hemorrhages/quadrant, venous beading, or IRMA. High
4 Proliferative DR Neovascularisation on disc/retina, vitreous/pre-retinal haemorrhage. Critical
